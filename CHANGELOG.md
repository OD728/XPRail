# XP Rail changelog

## 1.1

- New color schemes: **Gruvbox, Rosé Pine, Kanagawa, OneDark and Tokyo Night**.
- The Quality row now also has **Artifact** and **Heirloom**.
- The color rows are in the same order as in the author's other addons.
- The settings window is lavender instead of mint, the same look as the author's other addons.

## 1.0.1

- Minor fixes.
- The name in the AddOns list is now lavender, like the author's other addons.

## 1.0

First release.

- An experience bar that switches to your watched reputation at max level (or shows only one of them), in place of the game's bar.
- Rested XP and XP from completed quests shown as overlays ahead of your XP.
- Any length and thickness, horizontal or vertical, movable anywhere.
- Colors: the game's XP purple, class color, Classic bar colors, item quality colors, and the Neon, Catppuccin, Cyberpunk and Dracula sets. Reputation can keep its standing color.
- Thin black or gold border (gold like the default unit frames).
- Tooltip with the numbers, rested and quest XP, XP per hour this session and time to level.
- Options window: `/xprail`, right-click the bar, the minimap addon menu, or Options > AddOns > XP Rail.
- English and German.
